\section{Additional transport-instantiation diagnostics}
\label{app:transport-instantiation}

This appendix expands the locked full aggregate summarized in
\Cref{sec:transport-instantiation}.  The experiment protocol, configuration,
selection record, aggregate, and every generated artifact carry provenance and
SHA-256 hashes.  The figures use only the 50 evaluation seeds.  Smoke and tuning
results are absent.
