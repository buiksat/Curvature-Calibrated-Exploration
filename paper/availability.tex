\iflegacyextras
The repository contains the manuscript, theory derivations, implementation,
tests, frozen transport-instantiation protocol and configuration, optimizer
selection record, strict full aggregate, provenance records, and generated
tables and figures used in \Cref{sec:transport-instantiation}.  The aggregate is
bound to the complete raw-input inventory by SHA-256 provenance.  Raw run files
remain outside the committed source tree; no public raw-bundle URL is claimed.
The manuscript does not use the legacy experiments as evidence for
\Cref{thm:transported-ucb}; their scope is summarized in
\Cref{app:legacy-evidence}.  Detailed legacy figures, raw-bundle inventories,
and derived benchmark outputs are not part of the compact checkout; Git history
preserves them.
\else
The anonymous supplement accompanying this submission contains the frozen
transport-instantiation protocol and configuration, the experiment
implementation, the aggregation and artifact-rendering code, the optimizer
selection record, the strict full aggregate, the SHA-256 provenance sidecars,
the generated tables and figures used in \Cref{sec:transport-instantiation},
and a detached verifier that recomputes the reported statistics and regenerates
every published table, figure and CSV byte from the aggregate.  The aggregate is
bound to its complete raw-input inventory by SHA-256 provenance.

Two limits are stated rather than worked around.  First, the raw per-run
trajectory files are not distributed: they are large and were never committed,
so the supplement verifies the aggregate's inventory structure and digests but
cannot verify the raw bytes those digests name, and the verifier labels those
checks accordingly instead of reporting them as passes.  Consequently the
deterministic paired bootstrap, which resamples per-seed values, is not
recomputable from the supplement; its algorithm, level, resample count and
derived child seeds are recorded, and no per-seed values are synthesized in
their place.  Second, earlier studies in the same line of work were designed for
the one-sided dynamic theorem, do not execute the transported score, and record
none of its certificates; they are excluded from this submission and are not
evidence for any claim in it.
\fi
