\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Collection-time confidence can justify current-curvature scores through
explicit metric, operator, and solver certificates. The corrected center
supplies reference confidence along predictable parameter paths in a certified
region, without requiring nonlinear optimizer convergence. Historical error
and current bias remain explicit costs.

The construction's certified width is at least the direct reference width on
the same history, and exact actionwise transport reproduces that width
(\Cref{prop:reference-width-floor}).
This elementary comparison concerns the same information and center; it neither
rules out stronger confidence information nor orders the realized regret of
different policies. In particular, a larger bonus could change actions
beneficially; the width comparison does not exclude that possibility.

The completed follow-up exposes a substantial operational cost of the evaluated
Hessian certificate. All nine pointwise paired regret comparisons favor its
comparators, and exploratory log analysis finds that its bonus usually
determines its recommendation on the recorded states.
The primary bound is dominated by path inflation in this study. This differs
from base-component dominance in the original near-linear grid; neither
decomposition establishes a causal effect of drift across reward tasks.
The frozen policy's own bounds remain vacuous, so tightening transport does not
remove all confidence and approximation-error costs. All evaluated policies,
including the uncertified naive control, retain the reported floating-point
confidence and optimism diagnostics in the follow-up. These observations do
not establish the empirical necessity of the transport correction.

The model's fixed feature directions permit a relative-weight endpoint
certificate (\Cref{cor:tanh-relative-weights}). Its audit measures tightness on
existing histories, not a new policy's adaptive regret. A horizon-independent
endpoint certificate alone does not make the fixed-smoothness regret bound
sublinear: the separate confidence and bias terms remain.

\paragraph{Limitations and research questions.}
The guarantee is conditional on the pre-reward confidence, certificate,
measurability, and solver-width obligations in
\Cref{asm:abstract-transport,thm:transported-ucb}. The corrected-center
specialization additionally requires a fixed ex-ante reference model and
supplied linearization and misspecification envelopes. The empirical evidence
covers one synthetic model, a fixed teacher, shared prediction-based optimizer
selection, and dense direct solves. It establishes no practical advantage over
scalable neural-bandit methods. Floating-point checks are diagnostics, not
verified numerical enclosures. The original study's raw-data and runtime
provenance gaps remain distinct from the retained follow-up evidence, as
detailed in the reproducibility statement.

Two questions motivate further work: can stronger confidence information
justify useful current-geometry scores, and can their complete computational
cost improve on direct reference scoring? That cost must include the frozen
estimator needed by the corrected center. A new adaptive-policy comparison
would require a separately fixed protocol and fresh evaluation data.
