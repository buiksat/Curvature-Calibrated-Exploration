\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

A frozen ridge correction supplies reference confidence along predictable
nonlinear parameter paths without optimizer convergence. Confidence transport
connects that band to current curvature through separate metric, operator and
solver certificates. The reference-width floor and sharp symmetric factors
characterize this conversion; fixed-direction scaled tanh permits tighter
relative-weight endpoint certificates.

In the second study's near-linear design cell, where the frozen bound is below
the regret cap, endpoint transport and the frozen-reference policy had
equivalent mean cumulative pseudo-regret within a margin fixed before
evaluation, Hessian-path transport had higher mean cumulative pseudo-regret
than the frozen-reference policy, and UCB-GLM had lower mean regret; in the
most curved descriptive cell UCB-GLM had higher mean regret than linear UCB.

Four takeaways follow. First, on a fixed history transport cannot certify a
width below the reference width while it preserves the full reference band
(\Cref{prop:reference-width-floor}): it can make a current-curvature score
valid, but it cannot make the certified width narrower. Second, for scaled tanh
with $W\ge cT$ the uniform endpoint factor is $1+O(1/(cT))$
(\Cref{cor:tanh-endpoint-regime}), so the endpoint-transported bound keeps the
frozen bound's form, whereas Hessian-path transport had higher mean cumulative
pseudo-regret than the frozen-reference policy in every condition of both
studies. Third, in the curved cells of the second study, removing the
Taylor-error allowances lowered mean regret far more than switching among the
reference, endpoint and naive current widths changed it
(\Cref{tab:transport-v3-descriptive}). Fourth, no cell of either study has both
substantial curvature and a non-vacuous frozen bound, so a useful next
comparison needs stronger confidence information or a lower complete
computational cost, including the frozen estimator, and a specific
adaptive-policy hypothesis with a separately fixed protocol and fresh
evaluation data.
