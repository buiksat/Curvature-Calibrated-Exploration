%% Availability statement: distinguish historical and new evidence.
The research codebase retains the scientific implementation, protocols,
configurations, selection records, aggregation code, and manuscript tables.
The two controlled studies have different reproducibility limits.

\paragraph{Original near-linear study.}
For \Cref{sec:transport-instantiation}, the retained full aggregate, generated
tables and figures, provenance sidecars, and detached verifier support
aggregate-derived checks. The original per-run trajectories and per-seed
observations remain unavailable. Their inventory digests do not establish
access to or integrity of the missing raw bytes. In particular, the original
paired bootstrap cannot be recomputed from the aggregate alone; no per-seed
values have been synthesized to replace it. The historical dependency records
also disagree: requirements specify SciPy 1.15.2 while a retained host-specific
wheel link names SciPy 1.13.1. Two selection-time sources, the artifact renderer
and Buck build declaration, differ from their recorded digests. The verifier
reports these gaps explicitly. Fresh runs and dependency locks do not repair
the historical runtime or source provenance.

\paragraph{Prespecified follow-up.}
For \Cref{sec:transport-followup}, the retained portable export contains all
600 evaluation seed summaries, the development and tuning summaries, selected
optimizer, exact source and execution configurations, run provenance, terminal
bound decompositions, drift summaries, and a raw-input hash inventory. An
independent verifier reconstructs the reported terminal intervals from the seed
summaries. The pinned Python environment and source revision are recorded with
the run. Full raw trajectories are retained outside Git; the portable export
alone does not reproduce every roundwise audit or regret-curve interval. No
public raw-bundle URL or completed anonymous supplement is claimed.

\paragraph{Exploratory analyses.}
The mechanism and relative-certificate analyses use the already observed
follow-up histories. Their scripts, compact outputs, per-seed diagnostics, and
input hashes are retained separately from the frozen evaluation export.
Recomputing these analyses requires the corresponding raw trajectories; their
summary files alone are not a substitute. No new adaptive evaluation is
implied by rescoring recorded states. Ordinary floating-point replay and
source-hash agreement do not constitute verified numerical certification.

Earlier studies of the one-sided dynamic theorem do not execute the transported
score or record its certificate chain. They are excluded from this submission
and are not evidence for its claims.
