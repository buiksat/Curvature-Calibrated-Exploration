%% availability.tex
%% Availability statement for the self-contained TMLR manuscript snapshot.
\iflegacyextras
The repository contains the manuscript, theory derivations, implementation,
tests, frozen transport-instantiation protocol and configuration, optimizer
selection record, strict full aggregate, provenance records, and generated
tables and figures used in \Cref{sec:transport-instantiation}.  The aggregate is
bound to the complete raw-input inventory by SHA-256 provenance.  Raw run files
remain outside the committed source tree; no public raw-bundle URL is claimed.
The manuscript does not use the legacy experiments as evidence for
\Cref{thm:transported-ucb}; their scope is summarized in
\Cref{app:legacy-evidence}.  Detailed legacy figures, raw-bundle inventories,
and derived benchmark outputs are not part of the compact checkout; Git history
preserves them.
\else
The research codebase retains the frozen
transport-instantiation protocol and configuration, the experiment
implementation, the aggregation and artifact-rendering code, the optimizer
selection record, the strict full aggregate, the SHA-256 provenance sidecars,
the generated tables and figures used in \Cref{sec:transport-instantiation},
and a detached verifier for aggregate-derived statistics and generated
artifacts.  The verifier checks the reported aggregate summaries and regenerates
tables, figures, and CSV files from the retained aggregate; it does not rerun
the study or recover missing per-seed observations.  The aggregate is bound to
its complete raw-input inventory by SHA-256 provenance.

The following reproducibility limits remain.  First, the raw per-run
trajectory files are not distributed: they are large and were never committed,
so the detached verifier checks the aggregate's inventory structure and digests but
cannot verify the raw bytes those digests name, and the verifier labels those
checks accordingly instead of reporting them as passes.  Consequently the
deterministic paired bootstrap, which resamples per-seed values, is not
recomputable from the retained aggregate alone; its algorithm, level, resample count and
derived child seeds are recorded, and no per-seed values are synthesized in
their place.  Second, the inspected historical research snapshot specifies
SciPy 1.15.2 in its dependency requirements but retains a host-specific
SciPy 1.13.1 wheel link.  These historical records do not establish a single
matched runtime for the study.  The portable dependency lock prepared for
new runs does not recover the runtime used to generate the historical evidence.  Two selection-time source files, the artifact renderer and the Buck
build declaration, also differ from their recorded source digests.  The
verifier records both divergences explicitly; verifying the retained outputs
does not restore the selection-time source tree.  These dependency and source
provenance limitations are distinct from agreement between the manuscript and
the locked aggregate.

Earlier studies in the same line of work were designed for
the one-sided dynamic theorem, do not execute the transported score, and record
none of its certificates; they are excluded from this submission and are not
evidence for any claim in it.
\fi
