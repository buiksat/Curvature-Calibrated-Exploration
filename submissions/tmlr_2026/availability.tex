records. Its portable per-seed exports support terminal-table regeneration and

outputs. The supplement also contains the per-run records, analysis, development and
tuning records and implementation of \Cref{sec:transport-v3}. For the first
study, the portable per-seed exports support terminal means and paired
intervals, not all roundwise audits or intervals for the regret curves. Full
raw trajectories are not included in the compact supplement, and no
durable public URL for a raw bundle is provided; the exploratory
recomputations need those raw inputs, and hashes do not replace the bytes.
