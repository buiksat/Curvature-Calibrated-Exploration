\section{Scope of retained historical material}\label{app:legacy-evidence}
This submission concerns the confidence-transport theorem and the controlled
study in \Cref{sec:transport-instantiation}.  Its preceding appendices contain
the proofs and experimental diagnostics needed for those claims.  Earlier
one-sided dynamic-width results, auxiliary rate derivations, and their
experimental protocols remain in the research source history; they are not
additional contributions of this manuscript and are not needed to establish
\Cref{thm:transported-ucb}.

The earlier experiments did not execute the transported score or record its
path, operator, and solver certificates.  They therefore provide no evidence
for the current theorem or its numerical performance.  The present paper
makes no empirical claim for a large neural model, an approximate operator,
or an iterative solver: its reported study uses a 29-parameter model and
dense Cholesky solves.  These exclusions do not change the conditional
operator and solver interfaces proved in the theorem.
