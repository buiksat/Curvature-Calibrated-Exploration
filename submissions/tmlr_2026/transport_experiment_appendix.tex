\section{Additional original-study diagnostics}
\label{app:transport-instantiation}

This appendix expands the original locked full aggregate summarized in
\Cref{sec:transport-instantiation}.  The experiment protocol, configuration,
selection record, aggregate, and every generated artifact carry provenance and
SHA-256 hashes.  The figures use only the 50 evaluation seeds.  Smoke and tuning
results are absent.  The original raw trajectories were unavailable for this
revision; recorded hashes bind the retained artifacts but do not replace those
missing inputs or establish an independent raw-data reproduction.

\paragraph{Meaning of the cumulative bound decomposition.}
For a transport-Hessian trajectory, write
$P_T=(\sigma^2+G^2/\lambda)\gamma_T$ and
$\beta_t^{\rm stat}=\sqrt{\gamma_{t-1}+2\log(1/\delta)}+\sqrt\lambda S$.
The reported components are
\begin{align*}
 B_T^{\rm base}
 &=2\sqrt{P_T\sum_{t=1}^T(\beta_t^{\rm stat})^2},\\
 B_T^{\rm hist}
 &=2\sqrt{P_T\sum_{t=1}^T(\beta_t^{\rm corr})^2}-B_T^{\rm base},\\
 B_T^{\rm path}
 &=\sqrt{P_T\sum_{t=1}^T(\beta_t^{\rm corr})^2
       (1+e^{\bar D_t^Q})^2}-B_T^{\rm base}-B_T^{\rm hist},\\
 B_T^{\rm cur}&=2\sum_{t=1}^Tb_t(a_t).
\end{align*}
Their sum equals the sharp right-hand side in
\eqref{eq:sharp-transport-regret-new} for the experiment's
$\alpha_t=\kappa_{-,t}=\kappa_{+,t}=1$ and $\xi_t=0$.
The differences retain the same trajectory and the same potential factor;
they do not rerun a policy with a component removed.  The committed figure
CSVs report the mean of each component over 50 primary-policy trajectories
at each round.  At $T=1000$, dividing those terminal component means by the
corresponding mean sharp right-hand side gives base shares
$97.33\%,94.66\%,89.39\%,79.39\%$ and path shares
$2.01\%,4.04\%,8.16\%,16.27\%$ for
$D_{\rm target}=0.25,0.5,1,2$, respectively.
The remaining tightness summaries appear in
\Cref{fig:transport-original-components,fig:transport-original-bound-ratios}.
These joint cumulative quantities establish the decomposition;
marginal medians of $e^{\bar D_t^Q/2}$ do not determine it.
