               F.2   Novelty Relative to Prior Neural Bandits
               NeuralUCB/NeuralTS use historical-time full-gradient Grams in their stated algorithms, while NeuralLinear
               and last-layer Laplace restrict uncertainty to a learned representation or ﬁnal layer (Zhou et al., 2020; Zhang
               et al., 2021; Riquelme et al., 2018; Kristiadi et al., 2020). Matrix-free full-network predictive-variance solves also
               predate this work (Maddox et al., 2021). CC-UCB’s narrower adaptation is to replay every historical Jacobian
               at the current parameter, separate the predictable frozen conﬁdence Gram from that algorithmic operator, and
               expose centering, drift, transfer, and residual-checked CG terms. We claim neither the CG/CVP primitive nor a
               prior assertion of uniform full-metric superiority as new.
               The one-sided whitened result in Lemma 31 is the main technical step: optimism transfer needs no lower spectral
               comparison. The determinant formula is bookkeeping rather than a standalone rate, and the supplied-U closure
               is exactly the projected Gram in (87), not a concrete neural-training theorem. Appendix G compares NTK,
               regression, EKF/LO-FI, structured Laplace, and sketching alternatives.
